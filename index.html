Packers Poster

Sketch

My sketch is based off a poster made right after the 2011 NFL season, in which my team, the Green Bay Packers, won the Superbowl. The poster features two of our most important players that season, as well as the Lombardi trophy. The poster is very positive, showing the two players during the big game.

Poster

My updated version of the poster similarly shows two of our current most important players, Christian Watson and Jordan Love (Pookie), crying after the first round playoff loss in Chicago against the Bears. I unfortunately don't have the Lombardi to show in the middle, so I used the score from the loss.
